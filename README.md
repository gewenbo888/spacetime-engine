# Spacetime Engine · 时空引擎

A civilisation-scale, bilingual (English / 中文) exploration of the nature of space, time, gravity, causality and the structure of reality. From absolute Newtonian space to dynamic spacetime woven from energy, information and observation — relativity, gravity, black holes, quantum gravity, cosmology, and the question of whether spacetime is the deepest operating system of reality.

Ten interactive systems: the history of spacetime · space & geometry · the arrow of time · relativity & gravity · black holes · quantum spacetime · cosmology · information & observation · digital spacetime & simulation · the future spacetime engine. Plus a live three-lens **Spacetime Dial** (special relativity, gravitational collapse, scale), a seven-axis structure radar, and a four-lens **Reality Analyst**.

Everything switches between English and 中文 instantly via a global language toggle — no reload.

## Stack

Next.js 14 (App Router) · React 18 · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS. All visualizations are hand-built with the Canvas 2D API and SVG — no third-party rendering libraries. Space Grotesk + Spectral + JetBrains Mono + Noto Serif/Sans SC.
